# Editorial rules and content shapes

## Hard rules
- **Offers and commitments only, never their absence** (Doug, 2026-08-05). Drop any "uncommitted / no offers" line; don't single players out.
- **CONTENT.md is verbatim.** Record what Doug sent unchanged; list every edit under "Editorial decisions".
- **Light touch on alumni voice.** Fix punctuation (`court- getting` → `court — getting`), spell out state names in body copy, numbers under 10 as words, all-caps → sentence case. Never rephrase meaning.
- **Don't infer facts.** "From Bellevue East & Concordia" ≠ "played at Concordia" — ask, or say only what's stated. (Doug confirmed later; ask in the review note.)
- **No stale numbers.** If Doug sends no new funding figures, the giving section has no figures.
- **Title comes from Doug's message** (Aug "Another Summer in the Books", Oct "A Season of Fresh Beginnings").

## Flag for review (never decide silently)
The issue title · any words added to Doug's message (even when he invites edits) · facts you're unsure of · layout changes (e.g. a group photo).

## Spotlight schema
Name (maiden name in parens, as Doug writes it) · grad year · high school · college · current role + city · photo · italic one-paragraph intro (written by Claude, facts only from the source) · four Q&A blocks with these headings:
1. Describe your experience playing with the Crusaders. Any significant moments stand out?
2. What advice could you give to student-athletes about the recruiting process?
3. How has grassroots basketball changed since your days with the Crusaders?
4. What's life like now — a day in the life of <First name>!

Solo headshot → 100px circle next to the name. Group/family photo → full-width 520px image with a caption.
