Another Summer in the Books

All — hard to believe that we are through another summer of basketball, and on the doorstep to yet another school year! I hope you have all had a blessed summer of renewal.

I hope you enjoy this edition of the newsletter, which includes a summary of how our ETG teams fared this summer. We also shine the spotlight on two former Crusaders — Nick Ferrarini, a 2018 Grad now coaching at Washburn University, and Brooke Delano, a 2007 Grad now leading the volleyball program at Roncalli Catholic.

Much love to you all!

— Coach Woodard
