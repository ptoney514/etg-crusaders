ETG Girls — 17U

ETG 17U finished Adidas 3SSB play at 11–5 on the year and 13–4 across the last three tournaments of the season. The group put together a 5–0 showing at the I-35 Showcase in Wichita, beating Mokan 17U EYBL among other EYCL and 3SSB teams, went 8–4 in the July 3SSB events, and finished as Classic Runner-up.

Commitments:

  • Addison Medeck (Papillion South) committed to the Nebraska Cornhuskers in June — the third ETG player to commit to the Huskers in the last three years.
  • Zoe Beveridge committed to Wayne State, becoming the third player on the team to do so.
