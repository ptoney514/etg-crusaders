## Verbatim — Doug, 2026-10-06 (content)

*Amber Quinn (Sims) 2006 Crusader grad from Bellevue East & Concordia University. Family photo attached*

**My experience:** I had a really positive experience playing for the Crusaders. It was something that I loved in the moment, but I probably took for granted how big an impact it had on me as a person and a basketball player. The most memorable moments, as with a lot of teams, are the ones off the court- getting to travel and spend time with other strong athletes showed me parts of the country I never would have seen otherwise. On the court, it challenged me to adapt and find my role outside of my high school team; it can be easy to fall into a comfortable rhythm with a school team, and Crusaders pushed me to raise my expectations of myself and others.

**Recruiting:** The recruiting process can feel overwhelming and daunting, but my advice is to approach it with an understanding of what you value as a whole person. From a basketball standpoint, you want to look for coaches and systems that match your style of play and will continue to nurture your growth as an athlete; from an academic standpoint, you want to look at schools and communities that will bring you closer to your professional goals.

**Changes:** There are a lot of things that have changed about club basketball since I played; there are probably more opportunities for athletes to get on teams, which is great, but with more choices, there should be more caution. Parents and athletes should make sure teams have coaches who actually want what is best for their players as whole people.

**Life Now:** I live in Elkhorn, NE, with my husband and two kids, Cullen (11) and Raegan (9). I teach at Elkhorn North High School. When I started teaching, I coached high school basketball, but now my focus is on my kids and helping them to have positive experiences with the game.

**Short Intro:**

Happy fall to you all! I hope the school year has begun well for those of you with children, as well as the various fall athletic seasons that fill up many of your calendars! It is always a special time of renewal and fresh beginnings. Take the time to really enjoy it all, even in the sometimes hectic pace it imposes, because it will be so fleeting and they will grow so fast. Much love and joy to you all!!

Maybe also provide the link to give again...anything else you think appropriate or if you think my wording can be improved please feel free!! Thc Pernell
