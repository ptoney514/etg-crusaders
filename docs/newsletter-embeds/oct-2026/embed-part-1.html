A Message from the Coach

A Season of Fresh Beginnings

Happy fall to you all! I hope the school year has begun well for those of you with children, as well as the various fall athletic seasons that fill up many of your calendars!

It is always a special time of renewal and fresh beginnings. Take the time to really enjoy it all, even at the sometimes hectic pace it imposes, because it will be so fleeting and they will grow so fast.

In this edition we shine the spotlight on Amber Quinn (Sims), a 2006 Crusader grad from Bellevue East who went on to play at Concordia University and now teaches at Elkhorn North High School.

As always, thank you for supporting our Crusaders — if you're able to give, you'll find the link at the end of this newsletter.

Much love and joy to you all!

— Coach Woodard
